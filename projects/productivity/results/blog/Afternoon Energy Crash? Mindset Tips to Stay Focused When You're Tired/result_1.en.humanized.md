Ones mindset influences their perception of reality. Engaging in breathing goes beyond reducing stress; it serves as a potent technique to regain mental sharpness during the afternoon slump according to the respected mindfulness guru Thích Nhất Hạnh who suggests that through mindful breathing you can reestablish a connection, with the now and invigorate your mental well being."

Try out the box breathing method by breathing in for 4 counts and holding for another 4 counts before exhaling for 4 counts and taking a pause for 4 counts well; do this cycle four times to reset your brains pathways effectively and boost focus while reducing tiredness.. You can also give the energizing breath pattern a go. Breathe in rapidly through your nose to fill up your lungs completely and then breathe out quickly and fully; repeat this process quickly for one minute to feel energized.
