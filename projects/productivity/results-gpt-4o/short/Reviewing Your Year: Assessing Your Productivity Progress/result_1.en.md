# Boost Your Productivity Today!

Want to skyrocket your productivity? Try these simple strategies:

- Prioritize tasks using the Eisenhower Box to decide what's urgent or important.
- Use the Pomodoro Technique: Work in 25-minute bursts with 5-minute breaks to maintain focus.
