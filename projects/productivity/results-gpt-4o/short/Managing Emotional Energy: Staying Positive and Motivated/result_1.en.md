# Boost Your Productivity: Quick Tips

Looking to increase productivity? Try the "2-Minute Rule": If a task takes less than 2 minutes, do it immediately. Need help staying focused? Use the Pomodoro Technique: work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. Prioritize tasks using a to-do list, and tackle the hardest first when your energy is highest.
