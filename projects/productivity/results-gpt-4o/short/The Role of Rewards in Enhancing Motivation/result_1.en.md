# Boost Your Productivity in Minutes

Want to maximize your productivity daily? Start by prioritizing your top tasks each morning. Implement the Pomodoro Technique: work for 25 minutes, then take a short 5-minute break. This helps maintain focus and reduces burnout. Lastly, cut out distractions by setting boundaries for your workspace.
