# Boost Your Productivity: Simple Steps to Success

Struggling with your to-do list? Boost your productivity with these simple steps:  

1. Prioritize tasks by urgency and importance.  
2. Use the Pomodoro Technique—focus for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.  
3. Limit distractions by turning off non-essential notifications.
