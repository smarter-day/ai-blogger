# Boost Your Productivity with These Simple Tips

Feeling stuck? Boost your productivity by focusing on these three essentials:  
1. Prioritize tasks to tackle the most important work first.  
2. Use the Pomodoro Technique—work for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break.  
3. Eliminate distractions by creating a dedicated workspace.
