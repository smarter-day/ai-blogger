One effective strategy is the Pomodoro Technique. ✅ By breaking your work into focused sprints with short breaks, you can maintain momentum and stay energized. Try setting a timer for 25 minutes of focused work, followed by a 5-minute break. This method not only combats burnout but also improves concentration and efficiency, supercharging your productivity levels. 🔥

This clarity ensures you tackle tasks systematically rather than juggling too many at once, reducing stress and enhancing overall productivity.
