To kickstart your productivity journey, consider prioritizing tasks using the Eisenhower Box—an effective tool to distinguish between what’s urgent and important. These apps allow you to set reminders and deadlines, ensuring you focus on priorities.

Set a timer for focused work sessions followed by a short break. 🚀 This method not only enhances concentration but also optimizes your work-rest balance. If you find social media distracting, use website blockers during work hours to maintain your flow.
