Set clear, achievable goals and break them down into manageable tasks. 📝 By focusing on one task at a time, you can prevent feeling overwhelmed and ensure steady progress. This practice helps you stay disciplined and concentrated on the tasks at hand. 🚀 Creating a dedicated workspace and nurturing a habit of starting your day with a to-do list can also lay a strong foundation for increased productivity.
